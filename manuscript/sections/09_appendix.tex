\section{Proof of the Bound in Proposition~\ref{prop:linear}}
\label{app:linear}

Let $g = (1, 2k)$ and $S = 2k+1$.
Give slot $t$ the price
\begin{equation*}
  w(t) =
  \begin{cases}
    \lfloor t/2 \rfloor + 1, & 0 \le t < 2k,\\
    k + 1, & t = 2k,\\
    \lceil (4k - t)/2 \rceil, & 2k < t < 4k,\\
    0, & \text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation*}
A new robot serves its first four duties at slots $t_1 < t_2 < t_3 < t_4$ with $t_2 \ge t_1 + 1$, $t_3 \ge t_2 + 2k$ and $t_4 \ge t_3 + 1$, and its fifth duty after slot $4k$.
Checking the cases shows that it collects at most $S$.
Every slot must be served by $K$ robots, so the robots together collect $K \sum_t w(t) = K(2k^2 + 2k + 1)$.
Hence at least $K(2k^2+2k+1)/S = K(S^2+1)/(2S)$ robots are needed.
