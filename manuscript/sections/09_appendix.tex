\section{Tool Constraints of the Rate Relaxation}
\label{app:tools}

Let $y_{k,s,j}$ be the number of type-$j$ tools used per slot by the duties of type-$k$ robots at site $s$, with $y_{k,s,j} = 0$ if $j \notin \mathcal{J}_k$.
The rate relaxation \eqref{eq:rate} also contains
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
  & \textstyle\sum_{j} y_{k,s,j} = q_k \sum_i x_{k,i,s} && \forall k, s,\\
  & \textstyle\sum_{k,s} \tau^{\mathrm{tool}}_{k,s,j}\, y_{k,s,j} \le M_j && \forall j,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
and $y \ge 0$.
The first gives every duty its $q_k$ tools; the second counts tool time as \eqref{eq:rate} counts robot time.

\section{Proof of the Bound in Proposition~\ref{prop:linear}}
\label{app:linear}

Let $(\tau_0, \tau_1) = (1, 2k)$ and $P = 2k+1$.
Give time $t$ the price
\begin{equation*}
  w(t) =
  \begin{cases}
    \lfloor t/2 \rfloor + 1, & 0 \le t < 2k,\\
    k + 1, & t = 2k,\\
    \lceil (4k - t)/2 \rceil, & 2k < t < 4k,\\
    0, & \text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation*}
A new robot serves its first four duties at times $t_1 < t_2 < t_3 < t_4$ with $t_2 \ge t_1 + 1$, $t_3 \ge t_2 + 2k$ and $t_4 \ge t_3 + 1$, and its fifth duty after time $4k$.
Checking the cases shows that it collects at most $P$.
Every time unit must be served by $K$ robots, so the robots together collect $K \sum_t w(t) = K(2k^2 + 2k + 1)$.
Hence at least $K(2k^2+2k+1)/P = K(P^2+1)/(2P)$ robots are needed.
